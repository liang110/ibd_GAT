**Figure 1. Source-only, confound-audited framework for directional transfer.** The harmonized HMP2/IBDMDB and PRISM microbiome matrices are reduced to a frozen 126-feature contract before split-local source fitting. Clinical and identifier fields are removed, compositional transformation and supervised selection are fitted on the source training participants, and the selected features enter tabular and No-Prior graph-attention branches. Target samples remain locked until prediction. Bidirectional cohort transfer, PRISM origin-held-out tests, known-truth stress tests, paired participant-cluster uncertainty, topology nulls and an approximate capacity control define the interpretation boundary.

**Figure 2. Input scale, feature contract and exclusion audit.** (a) Per-sample library-sum ranges and medians in HMP2 and PRISM on a log scale, documenting the percentage-like versus proportion-like scale difference. (b) Feature accounting from the raw microbial matrices to the shared 126-feature contract and the empty exact-name metabolite intersection. (c) Source-only fitting versus target-only transformation for canonicalization, CLR/scaling, feature selection, denylist application and split assignment. (d) Immutable feature-order and canonicalizer hashes and the zero-mass exclusion audit; three HMP2 samples and no PRISM samples were excluded before CLR transformation.

**Figure 3. Participant leakage and known-truth controls.** (a) Seed-level AUROC under naive versus participant-grouped splits. (b) Paired participant overlap for the same seeds. (c) AUROC distributions for null, batch-only, signal-only, reversing-batch and topology-shift stress tests. (d) Target AUROC and stable-candidate counts across the domain-stability sweep, coded by whether all acceptance criteria were met. The synthetic panels are validation of the analysis procedure, not biological cohort validation.

**Figure 4. PRISM origin-held-out transfer.** (a) Paired source-validation and external-target AUROC values for the tabular origin benchmark. (b) Ten-repeat No-Prior GAT, elastic-net and random-forest AUROC means with source-split standard deviations. (c) Forest-style display of mean split-specific participant-cluster ΔAUROC estimates with across-split uncertainty; the manuscript summary averages split-specific estimates and interval endpoints and is not a pooled interval. (d) Average-precision changes between source validation and external target.

**Figure 5. Bidirectional transfer under the frozen microbiome contract.** (a) Split-level AUROC distributions for No-Prior Spearman GAT, elastic net and random forest in both directions. (b) Paired ΔAUROC intervals from the ten source splits. (c) AUROC versus average precision for each split and model. (d) Direction-by-model mean AUROC heatmap. The direction-specific contrast is the primary relational result.

**Figure 6. Topology ablations and edge-set diagnostics.** (a) Split-level AUROC values across no-edge, identity, rewired, Pearson and Spearman topologies in both transfer directions. (b) Mean AUROC by topology and direction. (c) Edge-set Jaccard overlap relative to the Spearman reference for Pearson and rewired graphs. (d) Fixed top-k construction assigns eight outgoing edges to every retained node; a conventional degree correlation is undefined under this construction and the audit placeholder is not interpreted as ecological conservation.

**Figure 7. Capacity-matched no-message comparator.** Paired transfer performance of the approximate no-message-passing multilayer perceptron and No-Prior Spearman GAT. The comparator uses the same source-only transformed input contract and similar hidden capacity. Differences are interpreted as evidence that generic nonlinear capacity does not fully account for the forward gain, not as proof of a unique causal graph mechanism.
