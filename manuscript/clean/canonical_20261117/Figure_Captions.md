**Figure 1. Evaluation workflow and information boundary.** A label-free intersection of cohort feature registries defines the shared microbial input universe. Within each of ten participant-grouped source fits, prevalence filtering, centred log-ratio transformation, scaling, mutual-information selection, graph construction and model fitting are restricted to source-training data. GATv2, elastic net, random forest, no-message-passing MLP and XGBoost are evaluated independently on locked external targets. Separate Real, degree-preserving Rewired and self-loop-only Identity input configurations define the topology sensitivity analysis. AUROC, average precision and Brier score address distinct assessment goals; uncertainty resamples target participants conditional on fixed source fits.

**Figure 2. Audit of input scales, shared features, analysis denominators and cohort provenance.** (a) Schematic comparison of nominal abundance scales in HMP2 and PRISM, distinguishing raw zero-mass observations from shared-space exclusions. (b) Feature harmonization retains the cohort-specific microbial registries and yields 126 shared candidate taxa; no metabolites match by exact name. (c) Included observation and participant denominators for HMP2 and PRISM. (d) PRISM discovery and validation strata and their IBD proportions. Provenance stratification does not remove recruitment-related confounding.

**Figure 3. Participant-overlap leakage and known-truth controls.** (a) Ten seed-level AUROCs under naive observation-level and participant-grouped splits; (b) participant overlap under the two split designs; (c) rejection of a planted clinical field; (d,e) target AUROC and acceptance under the five declared synthetic scenarios. These controls validate the data-processing and evaluation procedure, not a graph-based mechanism or causal microbial biology.

**Figure 4. Bidirectional paired model-family contrasts.** Graph attention (GATv2) minus elastic net, random forest, no-message MLP or XGBoost under HMP2→PRISM and PRISM→HMP2 transfer. Panels display AUROC, average precision and Brier contrasts plus the direction-by-reference matrix. Points are common-draw bootstrap means; whiskers show conditional 95% target-participant percentile intervals from 5,000 shared draws across ten fixed source fits.

**Figure 5. PRISM target-origin and target-scoring-unit sensitivities.** (a) HMP2→PRISM GAT−reference AUROC contrasts for elastic net, random forest, no-message MLP and XGBoost in discovery and validation provenance strata. (b) PRISM→HMP2 contrasts under visit scoring and participant scoring. The bootstrap unit is participant in both panels, whereas panel (b) changes the scoring unit. Panel (a) is a pooled HMP2-trained target-origin sensitivity and is distinct from direct source-origin transfer.

**Figure 6. Conditional graph-input topology contrasts.** Real−Rewired and Real−Identity AUROC differences, taxon-ID-aligned Real Spearman directed-edge Jaccard, and source-fit Real−Rewired ranges. Graph constraint checks apply to exported input-edge lists and do not establish causal biological edges or internal convolution self-loop handling.

**Figure 7. Model capacity and probability-error references.** Split-dependent parameter counts, paired GAT−MLP AUROC/Brier differences and GAT versus source-prevalence Brier references. The prevalence reference is descriptive; Brier is a probability-error metric and is not a standalone calibration test.
